Sondersort measures your behaviour across apps and websites and the time that you spend online. It helps you understand your usage across different kinds of apps e.g. social media, learning, entertainment, etc. This helps you understand your digital life and identify the opportunities to prioritize, reduce un productive time and optimise your digital life.
It also helps you craft your digital life and time that you invest. For instance, you can identify the apps / activites that you would like to cut down time on – e.g. on social media. How can I remove the app?

It is very simple – you can uninstall the app and remove it from your devices. Once the app is uninstalled, we do not have access to your phone. Thus, the data coming to us from you is stopped immediately.


In case you wish to rejoin the panel, you need to send us a request. We will send you an unique joining link which you need to register yourself.

How can I unsubscribe and how can I ask for removal of my data?
